# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners with basic computer-science knowledge who are building algorithm skill
through repeated practice, evidence-based review, and scheduled revisits.

## Product Purpose

EasyCode keeps coding, local tests, AI review, process replay, and later review
in one local-first training loop. Success means the learner can understand how
an answer was produced, what to improve, and when to return—not merely see an
accepted result.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and rigorous. The product should feel like a focused coach: plain
spoken, encouraging without cheerleading, and precise without internal jargon.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified streak pressure, generic SaaS dashboards, decorative AI imagery,
marketing-heavy copy, and implementation terms that learners must decode.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the learner inside one continuous practice loop.
2. Ground feedback in visible code, tests, and process evidence.
3. Explain the next useful action in plain language.
4. Preserve local ownership of code, history, and model configuration.
5. Prefer familiar product controls and quiet consistency over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-operable standard controls, clear focus
states, reduced-motion alternatives, and resilient layouts. Dedicated
accessibility features are not a separate product track, but baseline usability
must not regress.
